# Novalon House Studio 0.1.21

Furniture groups and a clearer editing workspace:

- Shift-click or draw a selection window to select furniture and fittings. Save a named group and select it later by clicking any member.
- Move or rotate a complete kitchen while preserving member sizes, internal spacing and intentional sink/hob nesting. Each drag is one undo step; Escape cancels it.
- Group and member locks protect the entire arrangement. Duplicate/delete/ungroup operate on whole groups. Save/reopen and level duplication preserve/remap the references.
- Resize the 2D/3D panes with the divider. Viewing controls expand/collapse, while Orbit, Avatar view and Walk inside stay directly accessible. Workspace settings are remembered locally.
- 2D, 3D, inventory, layout review and A3 plans share the same item geometry. Editing handles and group labels stay out of ordinary A3 exports.

The portable Windows x64 ZIP includes the app, the finished two-floor 10 × 8 metre house and a two-page A3 plan at 1:75. Extract the entire ZIP. Run the executable or `Open-grouped-kitchen.cmd`. No account or Internet connection is required to use the app.
